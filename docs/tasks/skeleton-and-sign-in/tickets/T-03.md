## What to build

Design sections 9.3 and 9.4 in full: the 30-minute idle timeout, sign-out,
the redirect of a signed-in person away from the sign-in page, the session
cookie's attributes, and the anti-forgery check applied in one place to every
state-changing request that carries a session.

`tests/implemented_rules.txt` gains IAM-R10, IAM-R11 and IAM-R12.

## Behaviours to prove

Spec scenarios
1. Every scenario of rules IAM-R10, IAM-R11 and IAM-R12 runs and passes:
   8 scenarios, 9 cases. That includes the idle boundary (29 min 59 s
   accepted, 30 min 00 s UNAUTHENTICATED) driven by the test clock, and the
   anti-forgery refusal with exactly one `ACCESS_DENIED` row and nothing else
   written.

Details the scenarios do not pin down
2. Only a request that resolves the signed-in person extends the idle window;
   a request refused as UNAUTHENTICATED does not revive an expired session.
3. A session that timed out is marked ended and cannot be used again even if
   the clock is later set back.
4. Two sessions of the same person time out independently.
5. The cookie has no `Max-Age` and no `Expires`; it is `Secure` only when
   `APP_ENV=production`.
6. The anti-forgery token is accepted from the `X-CSRF-Token` header or the
   `csrf_token` form field; a token belonging to another session is refused.
7. `POST /sign-in` works without a token; `POST /sign-out` without one is
   refused.
8. After sign-out the sign-in page is shown with 「您已登出」, and the old
   cookie is refused as UNAUTHENTICATED with 「請重新登入」.

Carried over from the T-02 verdict (pull request 9, findings 2 and 4)
9. A refusal page has a title naming its status (today
   `app/core/templates/error.html` renders an empty title because `status` is
   never passed to the template); a test checks it.
10. The test harness reaches into pytest-bdd names that are not public
    (`tests/conftest.py`, `tests/steps/__init__.py`,
    `tests/unit/test_spec_runner.py`). Pin `pytest-bdd` to the exact version
    the suite was built against, and say in a comment next to the pin why it
    is exact.
